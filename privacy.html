HabitRow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Applies to the HabitRow Android app (app.habitrow.tracker)

HabitRow does not collect any data. Not anonymised data, not “usage statistics”, not crash reports. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics of any kind.

You do not have to take our word for it. HabitRow does not request the INTERNET permission. Android will not let an app open a network connection without it, so the app is technically incapable of sending your habits anywhere. You can verify this yourself: Android Settings → Apps → HabitRow → Permissions, or run adb shell dumpsys package app.habitrow.tracker.

What is stored, and where

Everything HabitRow knows lives in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Your habits — their names, colours, schedules and reminder times
  • Which days you have checked off
  • Your settings — day-start hour, week-start day, theme

This data is readable only by HabitRow. It is never uploaded, never synchronised, and never shared with us or anyone else, because there is nowhere for it to go.

HabitRow itself never schedules background work, never starts a foreground service, and — having no INTERNET permission — cannot use network access regardless of what the network-state permission would otherwise allow.

There is no INTERNET permission and no advertising ID permission.

Purchases

HabitRow offers one optional, one-time purchase (“Pro”). It is not a subscription and it does not renew.

The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see and never receive your name, card details, billing address or any other payment information. HabitRow only ever learns one thing from Google Play: whether this Google account owns the Pro unlock. Google's handling of your purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

For refunds, use the Google Play refund process, or email us and we will help.

Backups

If you have Android's system backup enabled, Android may include HabitRow's habit data in your own Google account backup. That is a feature of Android, under your control, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup. Your Pro purchase status is deliberately excluded from that backup.

You can also export your data yourself, to a file you choose, from Settings → Back up to a file. That file is yours; HabitRow does not upload it anywhere.

Children

HabitRow is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and complete:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data.
  • Uninstall HabitRow. Android removes the app's private storage with it.

There is no server-side copy to request the deletion of, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If HabitRow ever starts collecting anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the app's release notes. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

HabitRow 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 18 日 · 适用于 HabitRow Android 应用（app.habitrow.tracker）

HabitRow 不收集任何数据。不收集匿名数据，不收集「使用统计」，不收集崩溃报告。没有账号，无需登录，没有广告，没有任何形式的数据分析。

这一点你可以自己验证，不必相信我们的说法。HabitRow 没有申请 INTERNET（联网）权限。Android 系统不允许没有该权限的应用建立网络连接，因此本应用在技术上就无法把你的数据发送到任何地方。验证方式：系统设置 → 应用 → HabitRow → 权限，或执行 adb shell dumpsys package app.habitrow.tracker。

存储了什么，存在哪里

HabitRow 所知道的一切都保存在你手机上该应用的私有存储区内：

  • 你的习惯——名称、颜色、频率和提醒时间
  • 你打过卡的日期
  • 你的设置——一天的开始时间、每周第一天、主题

这些数据只有 HabitRow 能读取。它们从不上传、从不同步、也从不与我们或任何第三方共享——因为根本没有可去之处。

本应用没有 INTERNET 权限，也没有广告标识符权限。

关于购买

HabitRow 提供一项可选的一次性购买（「Pro」）。它不是订阅，不会自动续费。

购买流程完全由 Google Play 处理。我们从不接触也从不接收你的姓名、银行卡信息、账单地址或任何其他支付信息。HabitRow 从 Google Play 只获知一件事：当前 Google 账号是否拥有 Pro 解锁。Google 对你购买行为的处理适用 Google 隐私权政策。

如需退款，请使用 Google Play 的退款流程，或发邮件给我们协助处理。

关于备份

如果你启用了 Android 的系统备份，Android 可能会把 HabitRow 的习惯数据纳入你自己的 Google 账号备份。这是 Android 的功能，由你控制，我们无权访问。你可以在系统设置 → Google → 备份中关闭它。你的 Pro 购买状态已被刻意排除在该备份之外。

你也可以自行导出数据到你指定的文件：设置 → 备份到文件。该文件归你所有，HabitRow 不会将其上传到任何地方。

关于儿童

HabitRow 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。由于本应用不收集任何数据，自然也不会收集儿童的任何数据。

删除你的数据

两种方式，都是立即且彻底的：

  • 在应用内：设置 → 删除全部数据。
  • 卸载 HabitRow。Android 会连同应用的私有存储一并删除。

不存在需要申请删除的服务器端副本，因为根本没有服务器。

本政策的变更

如果 HabitRow 将来开始收集任何数据，本页面会在该版本发布之前更新，并在应用的更新说明中明确指出。页首的日期始终对应当前版本。
