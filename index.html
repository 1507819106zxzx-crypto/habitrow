The widget is the product

Most habit trackers treat the widget as a bonus. This one is built the other way round: the widget reads straight from your data and repaints on every change — when you tap it, when you edit a habit, at midnight rollover, and after a timezone change. It will never show you yesterday.

  • Every widget size, unlimited instances. Never locked, never badged, never an upsell.
  • Streaks, reminders, calendar back-fill, backup and restore.
  • No account. No ads. No tracking.

Streaks that make sense

Streaks are recomputed from your history, never stored. Forgot Tuesday? Back-fill it and the streak repairs itself everywhere, instantly. Today never counts as a miss until it has ended.

If you want slack, pick a schedule that gives you slack — N times a week is a real option. We will never sell you a streak repair.

Private by construction, not by promise. HabitRow does not request the INTERNET permission. Android will not let an app open a network connection without it, so it is physically incapable of sending your habits anywhere. Check the permission list yourself.
